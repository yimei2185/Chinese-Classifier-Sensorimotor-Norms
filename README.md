# Chinese-Classifier-Sensorimotor-Norms
An open dataset of 11-dimensional sensorimotor ratings for 353 Mandarin Chinese classifiers, with external validation against noun-category profiles. 

## Study 1 data and screening

- Raw sessions: 523 across 24 questionnaire lists.
- Primary retained sample: 313 participants.
- Classifier inventory: 353 unique labels.
- Dimensions: vision, audition, gustation, olfaction, touch, interoception, leg/foot, hand/arm, mouth/throat, head, and torso.
- Rating scale: 0 = no experience; 5 = very strong experience.
- Screening rules: any failed Chinese-language attention check; list-specific completion time outside mean +/- 2 SD; more than 20% missing rating cells; or a longest identical-response run covering at least 90% of rating cells. Primary retention also required consent.
- The repeated questionnaire block for 重（chóng） was averaged within participant and dimension before calculating norms.


## Study 2 analytical sample

- 2,039 eligible analytical rows.
- 2,038 unique classifier-noun-concept edges after collapsing one simplified/traditional duplicate.
- 147 unique Study 1 classifier IDs and 758 corpus noun forms.
- Primary validation sample: 104 classifiers associated with at least two independently normed noun concepts.
